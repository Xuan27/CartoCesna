Ingredients

    1/2 whole chicken
    1/2 cup fideo pasta
    1.5 cloves garlic
    Salt (to taste)
    2 tomatoes
    1/2 tsp tomato bouillon
    1 carrot, chopped
    1/2 cup rice
    1/2 calabacita squash, chopped
    Cheese (for serving)
    Avocado (for serving)
    Chipotle chile (optional, for serving)
    Fresh tortillas (for serving)

Instructions

    Boil Chicken: Place the chicken in a large pot and cover with water. Bring to a boil and cook until the chicken is mostly cooked.
    Lightly Fry Fideo Pasta: In a separate pan, lightly fry the fideo pasta until golden brown.
    Blend Ingredients: In a blender, combine garlic, salt, tomatoes, and a little bit of tomato bouillon. Blend until smooth.
    Sift the Blend: Sift the blended mixture to remove any solids.
    Add Blend to Chicken: Pour the sifted mixture into the pot with the chicken.
    Wash the Rice: Wash the rice well under cold water.
    Add Carrots and Rice: When the chicken is about 80% cooked, add the chopped carrots and washed rice to the pot.
    Add Calabacita Squash: Once the stew is done, add the chopped calabacita squash.
    Finish Cooking: Turn off the heat and let the stew finish cooking with the residual heat.
    Serve: When serving, add pieces of cheese, avocado, and optionally, a chipotle chile. Serve with fresh tortillas.
