 Ingredients:

    1 pound chicken breast
    1 onion, quartered
    2 cloves garlic
    Salt to taste
    Mixed vegetables (carrots, green beans, corn, etc.)
    2 tablespoons media crema mexicana
    2 tablespoons Real mayonnaise
    Sandwich bread or tostadas
    Pickled chili peppers (chile en vinagre) 

Instructions:

    Cook the Chicken:
        Boil the chicken breast with the onion, garlic, and salt until fully cooked.
        Shred the cooked chicken and set aside.
    Prepare the Vegetables:
        Have your mixed vegetables (carrots, green beans, corn, etc.) ready.
    Mix the Chicken:
        In a bowl, combine the shredded chicken with 2 tablespoons of Mexican crema and 2 tablespoons of Real mayonnaise.
    Assemble:
        Spread the chicken mixture on sandwich bread or tostadas.
        Top with your preferred pickled chili peppers.
