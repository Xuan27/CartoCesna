Ingredients:

    1 cup white rice
    1 small onion, halved
    1 garlic clove
    2 cups hot water
    1 teaspoon chicken bouillon

Instructions:

    Slightly Fry the Rice: In a pan, slightly fry the white rice with the halved onion and the garlic clove. Fry until the rice is lightly golden, but not too much.
    Add Water and Bouillon: Remove the onion and garlic clove from the pan. Add the hot water and chicken bouillon to the rice.
    Cook the Rice: Stir well and cook until the rice is tender and the water is absorbed.
