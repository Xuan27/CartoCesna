 Ingredients:

    2 Poblano chiles
    1 cup cream cheese
    ½ cup Mexican sour cream
    Fresh cilantro (to taste)
    ½ cup milk
    1 tablespoon chicken bouillon
    Seasoned chicken
    1 egg
    Bread crumbs or panko
    Queso fresco
    1 pound spaghetti
    Salt
    Butter

Instructions:

    Roast the Poblano Chiles:
        Roast the chiles until the skin is charred.
        Place the roasted chiles in a plastic bag and let them sweat for 15 minutes.
        Peel off the skins.
    Cook the Spaghetti:
        While the chiles are roasting, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
        Add the spaghetti and cook according to the package instructions until al dente.
        Drain and set aside.
    Prepare the Sauce:
        In a blender, combine the cream cheese, Mexican sour cream, cilantro, milk, chicken bouillon, and peeled Poblano chiles.
        Blend until smooth.
    Simmer the Sauce:
        In a large pan, melt a bit of butter over low heat.
        Add the blended sauce and simmer gently.
    Combine with Spaghetti:
        Add the cooked spaghetti to the pan with the sauce.
        Toss to coat the spaghetti evenly with the sauce.
    Prepare the Chicken:
        Season the chicken with your preferred seasoning.
        Dip the chicken pieces in a beaten egg.
        Coat the chicken with bread crumbs or panko.
    Cook the Chicken:
        Fry the breaded chicken until golden and cooked through.
    Serve:
        Top the fried chicken with queso fresco.
        Serve the chicken alongside the spaghetti with Poblano sauce.
