 Ingredients:

    1 pound tilapia fillets
    1 Serrano chili, finely chopped
    ½ cup purple onion, finely chopped
    ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
    Juice of 4-5 lemons
    Salt to taste
    Your preferred salsa
    Tostadas

Instructions:

    Cook the Tilapia:
        Boil the tilapia fillets until cooked through but still firm.
        Cut the cooked tilapia into small pieces and place them in a deep dish.
    Add Ingredients:
        Add the finely chopped Serrano chili, purple onion, and cilantro to the tilapia.
        Squeeze the juice of 4-5 lemons over the mixture.
        Season with salt to taste.
    Serve:
        Mix all the ingredients together.
        Serve with your preferred salsa and tostadas.
