 Ingredients:

    1 bag of red chipotle chiles (Chipotle Rojo en Bolsa)
    2 tomatoes (Tomates)
    2 cloves garlic (Ajo)
    Salt to taste

Instructions:

    Boil the Ingredients:
        Boil the chipotle chiles, tomatoes (Tomates), and garlic (Ajo) together until they are soft.
    Blend:
        Transfer the boiled ingredients to a blender.
        Blend until smooth.
    Lightly Fry:
        In a pan, lightly fry the blended mixture.
        Add salt to taste.
