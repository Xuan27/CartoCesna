 Ingredients:

    1 chicken
    ½ pound roasted and salted pumpkin seeds (pepitas) (Walmart)
    3 tomatoes (tomates)
    8 guajillo chiles (chile guajillo)
    5 arbol chiles (chile de arbol)
    A small piece of cinnamon bark
    ¼ teaspoon cumin (comino)
    2 cloves (clavos)
    10 black peppercorns (pimientos)
    4-6 cloves garlic (dientes de ajo)

Instructions:

    Boil the Chiles and Tomatoes:
        Boil the guajillo chiles (chile guajillo), arbol chiles (chile de arbol), and tomatoes (tomates) together. Make sure to remove the veins from all the chiles. Do not boil the seeds/spices.
    Blend the Ingredients:
        Blend the boiled chiles, tomatoes (tomates), and pumpkin seeds (pepitas) until smooth. The mixture will look like a paste.
        Gradually add the pre-cooked chicken broth (caldo de pollo) to the paste while blending.
    Cook the Sauce:
        Pour the blended mixture into a pot and cook over low heat, stirring constantly for 10 minutes.
        Add more chicken broth (caldo de pollo) to achieve the desired consistency.
    Combine with Chicken:
        Add the cooked chicken to the pipian sauce once it reaches a mole-like consistency.
